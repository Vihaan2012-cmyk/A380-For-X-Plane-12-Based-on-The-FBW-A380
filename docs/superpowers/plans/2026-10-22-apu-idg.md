# APU and generator drive audit (PW980A, A380 DRIVE)

Date: 2026-10-22. Status: audit done; deep APU start re-fit applied the same day (see "Applied" at the end).

## Premise correction: the A380 has no IDG

The A380 has four 150 kVA variable-frequency generators (VFG) driven straight off the engine gearbox, plus two 120 kVA APU generators (IEEE Electrification Magazine, June 2014, "four 150-kVA VF generators (370-770 Hz) and two 120 kVA constant-frequency APU generators"). There is no constant speed drive. The overhead panel has DRIVE 1-4 pushbuttons (AUTO / DISC), FAULT on oil overheat or low oil pressure, DISC reconnectable only by ground maintenance, DISC forces the ENG GEN pb to FAULT (FlyByWire A380X docs, ovhd ELEC page). ECAM: ELEC DRIVE n OIL OVHT, ELEC DRIVE n OIL PRESS LO, ELEC DRIVE n OIL LEVEL LO. FBW still names its variables `ELEC_IDG_n` and `..._IDG_OIL_OUTLET_TEMPERATURE`; that is a naming leftover, behaviour is the DRIVE.

## Which model runs what

Two APU models run side by side in the plugin.

1. FlyByWire PW980 (fbw-common `apu/pw980.rs`, `pw980_physics.rs`, `electronic_control_box.rs`, `air_intake_flap.rs`), run through a380_systems `lib.rs`. This is what the cockpit shows (`APU_N`, `APU_EGT`, AVAIL, flap, bleed valve). Read only.
2. Plugin deep model `src/deep/apu/` (`Apu::step`, `live.rs`), publishes `DEEP_APU_N`, `DEEP_APU_EGT`, `APU_AVAILABLE`, failures, ECAM triggers. Its ECB protective trip does feed FBW's `APU_ECB_TRIP`, so a wrong deep model can shut the cockpit APU down.

Generators: FBW `Pw980ApuGenerator` and `EngineGenerator<DirectDrive>` are the cockpit truth; `src/deep/electrical/sources.rs` (`Vfg`, APU gen) is the plugin shadow. DRIVE oil is modelled twice: FBW `DirectDrive` (oil temperature only) and `deep/electrical/live.rs` (`ELEC_DRIVE_n_OIL_*`, reusing `deep/apu/oil.rs`).

## Findings (ordered by impact)

1. Deep APU starts in 8 s, FBW video timing and the cockpit model take about 40 s. The deep AVAIL (`APU_AVAILABLE`) and its ECB trips are in the wrong time frame. Cause: single-spool inertia `ROTOR_INERTIA_KG_M2` 0.028 with a starter at 593 A stall torque and a turbine that overwhelms compressor drag once lit.
2. Deep start EGT sits on its 900 C schedule limit; the observed peak is 570 C. Idle EGT is 603 C against 480-490 C.
3. Drive oil: FBW heats to 1.8 x N2 above ambient, so hot-day full-power oil exceeds the 200 C OVHT alert threshold with a healthy drive.
4. The deep APU generator and bleed loads are never fed (no `ELEC_APU_GEN_n_LOAD_W`, no `PNEU_APU_BLEED_DEMAND_KG_S`), so loading effects on EGT/fuel flow in the deep model do not exist in flight. They are exercised only by unit tests.
5. No altitude derate of bleed or generator capacity anywhere in the FBW core.
6. FBW's `STARTING_TO_RUNNING_N2_PERCENT` 83 and ECB `START_MOTOR_POWERED_UNTIL_N` 55 vs physics disengage 52: starter powered 3 N-points past clutch disengage, harmless.

## What was tried

In `src/deep/apu/` the start was measured with a throwaway test and tuned by hand, then reverted (git clean in `src/deep`):

| Change | 95 % N at | Start EGT peak |
|---|---|---|
| baseline (inertia 0.028, start limit 900) | 7.6 s | 900 C |
| inertia 0.15 | about 31 s, but crank alone 23 s, light-off to 95 % in 8 s | 900 C |
| inertia 0.08 and `EGT_START_LIMIT_C` 620 | about 20 s | 620 C |
| plus a start fuel-valve opening rate of 0.03 /s | about 21 s (no real effect; fuel is not the limiter) | 617 C |

Conclusion: one inertia value cannot give both the FBW crank phase (1.6 %/s) and the post-light-off phase (about 2.5 %/s), because the turbine's net torque after light-off is far more than needed. A single constant change is not enough, so nothing was applied.

## Proposed tuning (needs a design decision, not applied)

1. `src/deep/apu/params.rs`: `LIGHT_OFF_N_PERCENT` 12 to 8; `STARTER_KE_KT`/`STARTER_ARMATURE_RESISTANCE_OHM` so stall power is about 9.9 kW at 28 V (FBW 0.079 ohm), with disengage at 52 %; `ROTOR_INERTIA_KG_M2` about 0.08 to give a 1.6 %/s crank.
2. `src/deep/apu/power_section.rs` / `governor.rs`: bound post-light-off acceleration with a real acceleration schedule on N dot (limit about 2.5 N-points/s from 8 to 85 % N), not only an EGT limit; `EGT_START_LIMIT_C` stays 900 as the warning anchor but the schedule should target about 600 C control temperature.
3. Test target: from START with healthy battery at ISA sea level, N at 10 s in 14-18 %, at 20 s in 28-36 %, at 30 s in 56-66 %, 95 % N between 36 and 44 s, peak EGT between 520 and 650 C, no EGT above 900 C.
4. Loading: publish `ELEC_APU_GEN_n_LOAD_W` from `deep/electrical/live.rs` (`measured_apu_gen_load_w` exists but is not published) and `PNEU_APU_BLEED_DEMAND_KG_S` from `deep/pneumatic_ducts`; owner of those directories must do it. Acceptance: at 120 kW per gen plus 1.0 kg/s bleed the governed N rises 85 to 87 % N2 equivalent and EGT rises 40-80 C over no load (FBW's own "85 to 87").
5. FBW drive oil (read-only here; patch belongs in `fbw-common` `engine_generator.rs`): scale `TEMPERATURE_TO_RPM_FACTOR` 1.8 to about 1.2 so full-power equilibrium is about 135 C on a 15 C day and stays under 200 C to ISA+35; align `deep/electrical/live.rs` friction heating so the deep and FBW values agree.
6. FCOM-dependent items needing a real page before they are coded: APU start ceiling, bleed ceiling, generator ceiling, cooldown time, start-to-AVAIL time.

## Applied (22 Oct, deep APU only)

Files: `src/deep/apu/params.rs`, `governor.rs`, `apu.rs`, `power_section.rs`, `ecb.rs`, `starter.rs` (test numbers only).

- Starter: armature 0.061 ohm (with the 0.018 ohm battery, 355 A stall at 28 V, FBW 0.079 ohm total), Ke 0.00857 (back-EMF reaches 28 V at 52 % N, FBW "zero by ~52 %"). Self-sustaining / clutch speed 55 to 52 %.
- Light-off 12 to 8 % N (FBW `LIGHT_OFF_N2_PERCENT`).
- Rotor inertia 0.028 to 0.015 kg m2 (GENERIC, fitted to the FBW crank rate).
- New N-dot limiter (`governor::AccelLimiter`, schedule `start_ndot_schedule_pct_s`: 1.6 %/s to 30 % N, 3.0 at 36 %, 3.4 at 100 %), min-selected under the EGT ceilings with anti-windup.
- Start EGT control temperature `EGT_START_CONTROL_C` 640 C below 52 % N (`EGT_START_LIMIT_C` 900 kept as the warning anchor; running limit, trips, cooldown, overspeed unchanged).
- Idle EGT: `T4_DESIGN_K` 1150 to 995 K (GENERIC re-fit; the old value put no-load EGT at 603 C), `TURBINE_EFFICIENCY_FALLOFF` 0.45 to 0.15 (GENERIC; the part-speed turbine could not carry the core through 10-20 % N below 640 C).
- Gas path: turbine pressure ratio bounded by the compressor pressure ratio. Without it a near-unfuelled core still accelerated at 30-50 % N (the turbine expanded more than the compressor delivered).
- ECB low-oil trip armed from 52 % N, latched for the run (FBW: above 52 % N2); a slow start no longer trips on the normal low oil pressure of cranking.
- APU bleed demand ignored until AVAIL (bleed valve shut during the start).

Measured (`apu::tests::a_healthy_start_follows_the_fbw_pw980_timeline`, 28 V, ISA SL, no load): stall 354 A, light-off 8.x %, N 14.4 % at 10 s, 29.0 % at 20 s, 56.1 % at 30 s, 95 % N at about 42.5 s, AVAIL 42.6 s, start EGT peak 640 C, idle EGT 485 C. The test asserts the plan's bands plus idle 475-495 C. `cargo test --lib deep::apu`: 163 passed.

Known shape difference: the deep start EGT peaks just after light-off (10-15 % N) and falls through the start; FBW's curve peaks at 45 % N2. The peak value is in band; the shape needs a part-speed compressor/turbine map rework.

Not done here:
- Loads: `ELEC_APU_GEN_n_LOAD_W` is now published by `deep/electrical/live.rs` (uncommitted, another owner) and `PNEU_APU_BLEED_DEMAND_KG_S` by `deep/pneumatic_ducts/live.rs`. The loaded acceptance (85 to 87 % N2-equivalent, EGT +40-80 C at 2 x 120 kW + 1.0 kg/s) was not measured.
- DRIVE oil: the plugin-side value (`deep/electrical/live.rs`, `drive_oil` via `apu::oil`) heats a healthy drive by `400 W x N2` into a 60 W/K cooler, about 7 C above ambient at full N2, so it cannot reach the 200 C OVHT alert without a fault. The 1.8 x N2 figure is FBW's `DirectDrive` (read-only, `engine_generator.rs`); scaling it to 1.2 needs a patch to the FBW fork. The two values still disagree (deep ~22 C vs FBW ~195 C at 100 % N2 on a 15 C day); aligning them needs `deep/electrical/live.rs`, which another agent is editing.
