## What the real system does

From public material only (Airbus Safety First articles on the Runway Overrun Prevention System, the EASA/DGAC and Airbus landing-distance publications, the ROW/ROP descriptions published for the A380 and A350). Values below that are not stated publicly are marked as chosen.

- ROW (Runway Overrun Warning), in the air on approach. Active in final approach below 500 ft RA, gear down, until a floor of about 50 ft RA (below that the crew is committed, alert inhibited; chosen, configurable). It projects the touchdown point from the current path, then compares the landing distance computed for the actual runway condition and for the wet condition against the runway remaining after that point. Distance inputs: aircraft weight, speed at touchdown, wind, runway slope, runway condition, configuration. Two alerts: `RWY TOO SHORT` (the landing distance already exceeds the runway for the actual or dry condition, aural "RUNWAY TOO SHORT" repeating, amber on the PFD) and `IF WET: RWY TOO SHORT` (only the wet distance exceeds, message only, amber). Go-around inhibit on a positive climb rate.
- ROP (Runway Overrun Protection) on the ground after touchdown. Uses actual measured deceleration plus remaining runway to the end, available from touchdown until low speed. When the predicted stop point is beyond the runway end it escalates: aural "MAX BRAKING" (red message) if the pilot is braking manually below max; if autobrake is engaged the system itself applies maximum braking; then "SET MAX REVERSE" when braking is at max and reversers are not; then "KEEP MAX REVERSE" once the reversers are at max and speed has fallen under 80 kt. Applying thrust forward cancels it (go-around or touch and go). Not available in RTO.
- BTV (brake to vacate) coexists: BTV schedules a decel toward an exit; ROP has priority and overrides BTV with max braking when the end of the runway is at risk.
- Loss of function: hydraulic, electrical, ADIRS, RA, FWS audio, or OANS position loss makes ROW/ROP INOP (see table above). Independent of this crate.

Open: whether the real PFD message colours are amber for both ROW lines and red for the ROP lines. The task brief calls ROW amber; ROP lines are modelled red like other action-demand messages. Verify against a Safety First figure before shipping.

## Computations in the crate

`model.rs`:
- Condition decels (m/s^2) at max braking with ground spoilers: Dry 2.8, Wet 1.8 (both from FBW), Good 1.5, Medium 1.1, Medium-to-poor 0.8, Poor 0.55 (chosen).
- Without ground spoilers the brake decel is multiplied by 0.85.
- Slope: `+g * slope% / 100` (uphill positive).
- Reversers: `n * 75 kN * level * k(v) / weight`, `k` ramps from 0 at 30 kt to 1 at 100 kt (chosen). ROW does not credit reversers; ROP's capability estimate credits the available inboard reversers.
- Stopping distance integrates `v / decel(v)` in 0.5 m/s steps (speed-dependent reverser term), cap 5000 m.
- Ground roll = `v * 5 s transition + braking distance`.
- Touchdown ground speed = TAS minus 50 % of headwind or plus 150 % of tailwind (regulatory-style credit; configurable).
- Air distance to touchdown = `RA / tan(3 deg)` (glide path).
- ROP predicted stop = `v * max(0, 5 s - time on ground) + v^2 / (2 * max(measured decel, 0.2))`, cap 5000 m. In the first 5 s after touchdown the decel is `max(measured, 0.6 * capability)` so the transition cannot cause a false alert. The measured decel is the longitudinal accel low-passed at 0.5 s.

`logic.rs` (`RowRop::update(dt, &Inputs) -> Outputs`):
- Remaining runway = `LDA - distance_from_threshold - nose_offset`.
- ROW window: airborne, gear down, V/S at most +400 fpm, RA between 50 and 500 ft, runway known and position valid, not lost. Alert if `ground_roll(actual condition) >= remaining - air_distance` (latched until 50 m better); `IF WET` when the condition is Dry and only the wet roll exceeds.
- Stop points for the ND: `dry_stop_point_m` and `wet_stop_point_m` (ahead of the aircraft, airborne), `stop_bar_m` (predicted stop plus 40 m on the ground).
- ROP operative: on ground, GS above 10.28 m/s, not lost, not RTO. Overrun detected when the predicted stop reaches the runway end and no TLA is above 2 (forward thrust cancels).
- Automatic max braking latches when overrun is detected with autobrake active, holds at least 5 s, releases when autobrake drops, forward thrust is selected, or `1.2 * braking distance at capability < remaining` (this is the revert FBW left as a TODO). Slow speed does not release it. `btv_overridden` is set when the mode is BTV.
- Manual MAX BRAKING: overrun with no autobrake, thrust at idle. SET MAX REVERSE: overrun, inboard TLA above -20, GS at least 80 kt, at least one inboard reverser available. KEEP MAX REVERSE: both inboard at most -20, GS at most 80 kt.
- FWS gating copies `FwsAutoCallouts`: rollout gate with the 4.5 s bounce window, pedal override above 90 %, keep-reverse memory with the "aural played" input, RUNWAY TOO SHORT aural only in phases 8 to 10.
- Status word in the FBW bit layout (bit n is `1 << (n-1)`), `status_valid` false maps to SSM FailureWarning. `vars::word_raw` gives the `f64` for the L:var (`word + ssm * 2^32`).
- `vars::row_rop_lost` reproduces the Fcdc ROW/ROP lost formula for the host to feed `row_lost` / `rop_lost`.

## Tests (`tests/scenarios.rs`)

Dry long runway silent on approach and on a normal L3 rollout; short wet runway on approach gives RWY TOO SHORT with aural, amber message and bit 15; dry short-if-wet gives IF WET only; ROW inhibits (go-around climb, gear up, below 50 ft, above 500 ft, lost); ROW clears on a longer runway; slow deceleration with autobrake commands max braking and SET MAX REVERSE; manual braking goes MAX BRAKING, then SET MAX REVERSE with pedals, then KEEP MAX REVERSE (memory cleared by the played flag); no false alert in the transition; forward thrust inhibits; ROP lost and RTO inhibit; latch release and 5 s minimum hold; BTV override; bounce window length; model ordering (dry < wet < poor, slope, reversers, weight, wind); lost flags and word encoding.

## Plugin hooks

Nothing is wired yet. The crate is the logic; the hosts have to connect it.

Inputs, per frame:
- Runway: LDA, slope, condition from `crates/oans` (`ropsDetectedRunwayLda`, `L:A32NX_OANS_RWY_LENGTH`) and the OIT/EFB landing performance runway condition (the plugin has the TLR runway state from the SimBrief/OIT path). `dist_from_threshold_m` and `position_valid` from the oans26 position computer, same source as `L:A32NX_OANS_BTV_REMAINING_DIST_TO_RWY_END` (oans26 should expose it to this crate or the host should convert: `dist_from_threshold = LDA - remaining`).
- Aircraft: RA, V/S, GS, TAS, headwind component (wind vars), longitudinal accel, weight (`deep_systems` payload/fuel), on-ground and gear down (LGCIU), ground spoilers, FWS flight phase (`crates/fws`).
- Braking: pedal inputs (`A32NX_LEFT_BRAKE_PEDAL_INPUT`, `..._RIGHT_...`), autobrake armed mode (`A32NX_AUTOBRAKES_ARMED_MODE`) and governor engaged (`A32NX_AUTOBRAKES_ACTIVE`).
- Reversers: `A32NX_AUTOTHRUST_TLA:1..4`, reverser INOP flags from the engine failure model.
- Lost: `row_lost` / `rop_lost` from `vars::row_rop_lost` fed by the FCDC discretes, or read `L:A32NX_ROW_ROP_LOST` if the FCDC host exports it.

Outputs:
- FWS alerts: write `L:A32NX_ROW_ROP_WORD_1` via `vars::word_raw(out.status_word, out.status_valid)`. This is the var `FwsAutoCallouts` and `src/ecam_patches.rs` already read, so the existing FWS path keeps working; `crates/fws` can either keep its own gating (use only the word) or consume `Outputs::aurals` directly and skip it.
- Aural path: set `L:A32NX_AUDIO_ROP_MAX_BRAKING`, `..._ROW_SET_MAX_REVERSE`, `..._ROP_KEEP_MAX_REVERSE`, `..._ROW_RWY_TOO_SHORT` from `out.aurals` (names in `vars.rs`; `crates/fws/src/core.rs` already lists two of them). Write `A32NX_...KEEP_MAX_REVERSE` 0 when the sound manager reports it played, and feed that back as `keep_max_reverse_played`.
- Autobrake command: `out.auto_max_braking` must force the brake demand to full in the hydraulic/brake path (the equivalent of `rop_max_braking_requested` in `autobrakes.rs`); in this plugin that is the autobrake governor in `deep_systems` / brake model. `btv_overridden` tells the BTV scheduler to drop its schedule.
- PFD/ND display vars: `out.pfd_messages` (text and `level()`), and the distances `L:A32NX_OANS_BTV_WET_DISTANCE_ESTIMATED`, `..._DRY_DISTANCE_ESTIMATED`, `..._STOP_BAR_DISTANCE_ESTIMATED` from `wet_stop_point_m`, `dry_stop_point_m`, `stop_bar_m`. The native PFD in `a380-displays` has no ROW/ROP message slot yet; it needs one above the speed tape (amber ROW, red ROP) and the ND needs the stop-bar marker already used by BTV.
- Inop: ECAM INOP SYS 320300022 / 320300025 stay driven by the existing `rowLost` / `ropLost`.
