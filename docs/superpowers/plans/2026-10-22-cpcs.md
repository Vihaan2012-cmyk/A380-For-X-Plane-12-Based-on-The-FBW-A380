# Cabin pressure control (CPCS) audit and extras (crates/cpcs_extra)

Scope: public A380 behaviour only. Sources read: FlyByWire A380X Rust systems (`a380_systems/src/air_conditioning`, `fbw-common .../cabin_pressure_controller.rs`, `pressure_valve.rs`, `cabin_air.rs`), the A380X flight warning system TypeScript, the plugin (`src`, `crates`). The local FCOM text was not read. Numbers marked "tuning" are our own parameterisation, not certified figures.

## What FBW already runs (and the plugin runs unmodified)

- Four CPIOM B pressure control applications (`CabinPressureControlSystemApplication`) pick the schedule and the cabin V/S and altitude targets; four outflow valve control modules (OCSM) each hold an automatic partition, a safety and override partition, an emergency pressurisation partition and two channels on separate buses.
- Schedule state machine: Ground, TakeOff, Climb, Cruise, Descent, Abort, with the timers and thresholds in `cabin_pressure_controller.rs`. Climb target altitude is the polynomial in aircraft altitude, with the FMS cruise altitude used when the lateral mode is 20; rates are clamped to +1000/-350 fpm (descent +500), TakeOff holds -300 fpm until 0.1 psi, Abort returns to departure elevation at -500 fpm, ground opens the OFVs after 55 s.
- Reference pressure: ISA above 5000 ft from departure and landing fields, ADIRS baro or destination QNH below, ISA when manual.
- Landing elevation: read from `A32NX_FM1_LANDING_ELEVATION` (ARINC) with the descent schedule aiming the cabin at landing elevation minus 0.1 psi equivalent.
- Manual mode: MAN ALTITUDE and MAN V/S CTL pushbuttons with knobs (V/S clamped -1500..+2500), safety partition derives the target V/S (500 up, -300 down default, capped by the remaining distance).
- OFV: four valves, 4 s auto travel, 55 s battery-powered manual motor model, PID per valve; EPP drives the valves at 6400 fpm above 8.8 psi, closes them above 22000 ft cabin and with DITCHING; backup landing elevation 7000 ft when every automatic target is lost.
- Safety: negative relief valve (opens at -0.725 psi, 1 s travel), positive protection through the OFV EPP, overpressure relief valve dump in the VCM.
- Faults and redundancy: per application, per partition, per channel failures with breaker and bus dependence, cross-communication of the V/S target between OCSMs, loss of 3+ CPIOM B forces the OCSM backup, `PRESS_OCSM_n_CHANNEL_m_FAILURE`, CPC discrete words (excess altitude bit 13, excess diff, negative diff, low diff, residual), manual partition `PRESS_MAN_*` signals.
- Warnings (FwsCore): EXCESS CAB ALT, EXCESS DIFF PRESS, EXCESS NEGATIVE DIFF PRESS, EXCESS RESIDUAL DIFF PRESS, AUTO CTL FAULT, CTL REDUNDANCY LOST, DIFF PRESS HI/LO, INHIBITED BY DOORS, OUTFLW VLV CTL 1-4 and combined, SYS FAULT, with the EXCESS CAB ALT checklist containing the EMER DESCENT items. The plugin adds CAB PRESS SENSORS FAULT, MAN CTL FAULT, OUTFLW VLV CTL FAULT, DIFF PRESS HI/LO and EXCESS NEGATIVE DIFF PRESS from `deep` (`ecam/fbw/ata21_22_23.rs`, `sensors/live_discrete.rs` `DEEP_CPC_n_*`).
- Plugin consumers: oxygen module (cabin altitude, mask deployment at 14000 ft), doors (delta P gate), cabin water/waste (delta P), deep truth (`cabin_pressure_pa` from the CPC1 delta P word), study PRESS page, breakers and failure table (OCSM, CPCS app, hull breach 21000).

## Exists / missing per behaviour

| Behaviour | Status | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Auto cabin altitude and rate schedules (climb, cruise, descent, abort, ground, take-off) | Exists | FBW CPIOM B, unmodified |
| Automatic landing elevation from the FMS | Exists in FBW, missing in the plugin feed | The native FMS crates (`fms*`) publish none of `FM1_LANDING_ELEVATION`, `DESTINATION_QNH`, `AIRLINER_CRUISE_ALTITUDE`; only FBW's JS FMS does. Invalid word falls to 0 ft in FBW, which makes the descent schedule aim at sea level |
| Landing elevation fallback (FMS lost, no destination) | Missing | `landing::LandingElevationResolver`: hold last FMS value, else departure field, cleared at touchdown (our design) |
| Manual mode, manual V/S and manual altitude | Exists in FBW | Panel L:vars exist. Cockpit knob binding was listed unresolved in the old CTRL-006 study; stepping/clamping not in plugin |
| Manual knob stepping and advisory | Missing | `manual::Knob`, `Panel`, `advisory` (target unreachable at selected rate, high cabin rate expected near the pressure limits) |
| Manual outflow valve direct control | Not present on the A380 panel | FBW models the 55 s battery motor but never selects it (`outflow_valve.update(.., false)`); inside FBW source, read only, so only described below |
| Outflow valve behaviour | Exists | 4 valves, PID, EPP, closed-on-ground logic |
| Failed OCSM drives its valve closed | FBW behaviour to review | `open_allowed` is false when both channels fail, so a failed unit signals closed. Upstream only |
| Safety valve (negative relief) | Exists | opens below -0.725 psi |
| Positive safety relief valve | Missing as a state | FBW protects through EPP and OFV only; `relief::ReliefValve` gives open fraction, leak area and stuck faults for sound and the PRESS page |
| Controller faults and redundancy | Exists | applications, partitions, channels, cross-communication, backup landing elevation |
| Active controller selection, alert derivation | Exists | FwsCore (`cpcsToUseId`, `pressRedundLost`, `ocsmAutoCtlFault`) |
| Excess cabin altitude warning | Exists | CPC discrete bit 13 plus manual partition signal |
| Excess cab alt to emergency descent link | Missing | Checklist text exists; nothing joins the warning to mask expectation, PA announcement, idle/speed brake recommendation, descent established and recovery. `excess::Monitor` |
| Cabin pressure effects in sound | Missing | Sound pack has no pressurisation sound. `sound::CabinPressureSound` |
| Time to 14000 ft estimate | Missing | `excess::time_to_mask_alt_s` |

## Crate `crates/cpcs_extra`

No dependencies, empty `[workspace]`, pure logic. 44 unit tests, `cargo test` with `CARGO_TARGET_DIR=E:/fbw-build/target`.

- `isa`: pressure altitude and pressure at altitude (ISA, reference pressure).
- `landing`: `landing_elevation_ft` (touchdown zone, then threshold, then airport), `landing_elevation_word` (32 ft resolution, -2000..14000, SSM NCD when no destination), `destination_qnh_hpa`, `cruise_altitude_ft` (zero when implausible), `LandingElevationResolver` with `Source` code.
- `excess`: `Monitor` with phases Inactive, Recommended, Established, Recovered; debounced trigger, escalation only when the cabin is not recovering (cabin at or above 14000 ft, within 60 s of it, or delta P under 1 psi above 9000 ft), PA announcement pulse, idle thrust and speed brake recommendations, MAX level 10000 ft target, clear after 30 s, reset on ground.
- `manual`: `Knob`, `Panel` (V/S -1500..2500 by 50, altitude -2000..14000 by 100), `advisory`.
- `relief`: positive and negative relief valve with 1 s travel and stuck open/closed faults.
- `sound`: outflow hiss gain and pitch (differential pressure, OFV opening, flight only), safety valve, ear pop events (600 ft climb, 300 ft descent), decompression bang on a drop faster than 1.5 psi/s, roar decaying over 12 s (held while a hull breach is active), residual pressure hiss on door opening, cabin density gain (1.0 to 0.72).
- `ids`: every L:var and dataref name used by the hooks.

## Plugin hooks (not applied; plugin src and deep crates were not edited)

1. Native FMS to CPCS: each tick in the FMS output stage write `ids::FMS_LANDING_ELEVATION` as an ARINC 429 word from `landing::landing_elevation_word(landing::landing_elevation_ft(dest))`, `FMS_DESTINATION_QNH` from `destination_qnh_hpa`, `FMS_CRUISE_ALTITUDE` from `cruise_altitude_ft`. `FMA_LATERAL_MODE` stays with the FMA owner (value 20 gates the cruise-altitude climb target).
2. Landing elevation fallback: in the same stage, run `LandingElevationResolver::update(fms_ft, on_ground, cabin_alt_ft)` and write its `elevation_ft` instead of 0 when the FMS value is invalid; export `Source::code()` to `OUT_LANDING_ELEVATION_SOURCE` for the study page.
3. Emergency descent link: after the systems tick read `CPCS_DISCRETE_WORD[n]` (bit 13) of the CPCS in use or `MAN_EXCESSIVE_CABIN_ALTITUDE`, `CPCS_CABIN_ALTITUDE/VS/DELTA_PRESSURE`, ADIRS altitude and vertical speed, on-ground; feed `excess::Monitor::update`; publish `OUT_EMER_DESCENT_PHASE`, `OUT_EMER_DESCENT_ANNOUNCE` (triggers the PA sound in `sound_pack.rs`), `OUT_MASKS_EXPECTED`, `OUT_TIME_TO_MASK_ALT_S`. The oxygen module keeps owning the real mask drop at 14000 ft.
4. Manual panel: bind the overhead knobs to `manual::Panel` (click or wheel to `Knob::click`, drag to `Knob::drag`) and write `OVHD_ALT_KNOB`, `OVHD_VS_KNOB`; seed the altitude knob with `seed_altitude_from_cabin` when MAN ALT is pressed. Study page can show `manual::advisory`.
5. Sound: each frame call `CabinPressureSound::update` with the PRESS words (`CPCS_CABIN_VS`, `CPCS_DELTA_PRESSURE`, `OUTFLOW_VALVE_OPEN_PERCENTAGE`, `SAFETY_VALVE_OPEN_PERCENTAGE`), on-ground, any cabin door open (doors.rs), and the `RapidDecompression` failure; write the `SND_*` datarefs and map them to FMOD events in the x_A380 bank (new events needed in the sound pack: hiss loop, valve loop, pop, bang, roar loop, door hiss).
6. Relief valve state: step `relief::ReliefValve` with the CPC delta P and publish its percent for the PRESS page and for sound; leak area is advisory only because FBW's cabin simulation takes its leak areas internally.

## Small changes to deep_systems or FBW that cannot be done from the plugin

- `crates/deep_systems` has no pressurisation code; nothing to edit there.
- FBW `outflow_valve_control_module.rs` line 141: `open_allowed` includes `!BothChannelsFault`, so a failed OCSM drives its valve closed. A failed unit should freeze its valve (signal `None`) and let the other three compensate. Same file: `overpressure_relief_valve_inhibit` returns `false` (Fixme); `mod.rs` negative relief valve has "TODO Add check for failure". Upstream-only changes.
- FBW `mod.rs` `outflow_valve.update(.., false)`: the manual motor is never selected; select it when both OCSM channels are lost and the battery bus is powered.
- Landing elevation `unwrap_or_default()` in `cabin_pressure_controller.rs` and `cpiom_b.rs` treats an invalid word as 0 ft; the plugin-side fallback above avoids this without touching FBW.

## Not verified

Nothing was run in X-Plane. Thresholds for the emergency descent link, ear pop distances, hiss shape and the knob ranges are tuning values from public descriptions of behaviour, to be adjusted by listening and flying.
