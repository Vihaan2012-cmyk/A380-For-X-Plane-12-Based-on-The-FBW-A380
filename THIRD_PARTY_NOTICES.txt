2. Microsoft Flight Simulator
   FlyByWire's A380X is an add-on for Microsoft Flight Simulator. This port
   contains no Microsoft Flight Simulator models or textures: those are
   FlyByWire's own. Its instruments currently run on xp-kit, an avionics UI
   library compatible with Microsoft's MSFS avionics framework. A similarity
   check in October 2026 found that xp-kit reproduces parts of that
   framework's implementation. It is being replaced by an independently
   written library, and its source has been withdrawn from this repository.
   Microsoft Flight Simulator (c) Microsoft Corporation.

7. Sounds
   Engine, ground, weather, warning, callout and switch sounds (fmod/):
   A380RR_Sp v1.2, demo "P-HAGER" edition (for Peter Hager's A380), by
   FTSiM+Sounds, https://www.ftsimplus.net/a380rr.html. The pack is not in
   the release: during installation the installer downloads the demo from
   FTSiM+'s own link (or uses your own copy of A380_Sp(RR)(P)(C).zip) and
   adapts it to this aircraft on your computer (its sound parameters
   renamed to this aircraft's, and this aircraft's own sound positions
   applied to its A388.snd). FTSiM+'s full version is on their Patreon,
   https://www.patreon.com/FTSIM.

8. Laminar Research
   The aircraft's .acf is built on Laminar Research's Airbus A330-300, and
   its wing uses that aircraft's airfoils. Neither is in the release: the
   installer builds the .acf from your own X-Plane 12's A330.acf and this
   aircraft's own settings, and copies the airfoils from your own X-Plane.
